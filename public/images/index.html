About Paima

Paima is a collection of 777 vibrant arapaima NFTs on the Sui blockchain, celebrating one of the Amazon's most awe-inspiring creatures.

The arapaima, a majestic fish native to the Amazon River, can grow over 10 feet long, embodying the beauty and mystery of the rainforest.

Conservation Commitment

A portion of all proceeds from this collection will be donated to the Rainforest Foundation, directly supporting efforts to protect the Amazon and its countless species.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Paima NFT Collection? +

Paima is a limited collection of 777 unique digital arapaima fish living on the Sui blockchain. Each NFT is uniquely generated with various traits and characteristics of different rarities, inspired by the majestic arapaima fish native to the Amazon river.

How can I mint a Paima NFT? +

You can mint Paima NFTs directly on our website by connecting your Sui-compatible wallet and clicking the "Mint" button. The minting process requires SUI tokens for the mint price plus gas fees.

How does Paima support conservation? +

A portion of all proceeds from the Paima NFT collection will be donated directly to the Rainforest Foundation to support their efforts in protecting the Amazon rainforest and its wildlife, including the arapaima fish. We will provide transparent tracking of all donations.

When is the mint date? +

The public mint will begin on March 15, 2025, at 3 PM UTC. Whitelist members will have early access 24 hours before the public mint.

What blockchain is Paima built on? +

Paima NFTs are minted on the Sui blockchain, chosen for its energy efficiency and low environmental impact compared to other blockchain networks.
